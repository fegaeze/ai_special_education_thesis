### Compare-Less: Larger Quantity Unknown

[
"Today 9 children in our class wore boots to school. That’s 5 fewer than the number of students who wore shoes. How many students wore shoes to school?",
"The planet Uranus has 5 large moons. That’s 17 fewer than the number of smaller moons it has. How many smaller moons does the planet Uranus have?",
"A library has 24 mystery books. This is 13 fewer than the number of science fiction books in the library. How many science fiction books are there?",
"A team scored 28 points in the first half of a game. They scored 9 fewer points in the first half than in the second half. How many points did they score in the second half?",
"A bakery sold 45 cupcakes on Monday. This was 15 fewer than the number of cupcakes sold on Tuesday. How many cupcakes were sold on Tuesday?",
"A farmer has 32 cows. This is 18 fewer than the number of chickens on the farm. How many chickens are on the farm?",
"At a concert, 120 people were seated in the front rows. That was 55 fewer than the number of people in the back rows. How many people were in the back rows?",
"Emma read 7 books this month. That’s 5 fewer than the number of books Olivia read. How many books did Olivia read?",
"There are 12 ducks in the pond. That’s 6 fewer than the number of geese in the pond. How many geese are in the pond?"
]
