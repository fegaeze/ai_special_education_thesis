### Combine: The part is unknown

1. There were 32 penguins on an iceberg. Five of the penguins were sleeping. How many penguins were not sleeping?
2. Nathan baked 36 cookies for his class. Twenty-five of them were shaped like crescent moons. How many of the cookies were round?
3. Nika has 10 teddy bears. Three of them have bows. How many of her bears do not have bows?
4. There were 26 people on the Ferris wheel. Eighteen of them were kids. How many adults were on the Ferris wheel?
5. There are 85 students eating lunch in the cafeteria. Thirty-eight of the students brought their lunch from home. How many students did not bring their lunch from home?
6. Desmond bought 20 apples at the store. He got 6 red apples. How many green apples did he buy?
7. Fifteen children are outside playing in the snow. Thirteen of them are sledding down a hill. How many children are not sledding down the hill?
8. Javier is watching 8 monkeys at the zoo. Six of the monkeys are climbing in the tree. How many of the monkeys are not climbing in the tree?
9. Felix checked out 12 books from the library. He checked out 5 fiction books and some nonfiction books. How many nonfiction books did he check out?
