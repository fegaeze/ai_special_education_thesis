### Compare-Less: Difference Unknown

1. Alice has 5 apples, and Bob has 7 apples. How many fewer apples does Alice have than Bob?
2. A store sold 30 hats on Monday and 24 hats on Tuesday. How many fewer hats did the store sell on Tuesday than on Monday?
3. A bakery made 50 cupcakes on Friday and 35 cupcakes on Saturday. How many fewer cupcakes did the bakery make on Saturday than on Friday?
4. Oliver has 25 marbles, and Liam has 40 marbles. How many fewer marbles does Oliver have than Liam?
5. A school has 200 students in the morning class and 180 students in the afternoon class. How many fewer students are in the afternoon class than in the morning class?
6. A car traveled 200 miles on Monday and 150 miles on Tuesday. How many fewer miles did the car travel on Tuesday than on Monday?
7. There were 50 people at the concert on Friday and 35 people on Saturday. How many fewer people attended the concert on Saturday than on Friday?
8. The zoo has 70 elephants and 55 giraffes. How many fewer giraffes does the zoo have than elephants?
9. Sarah’s bookshelf holds 50 books, and Emily’s holds 40 books. How many fewer books does Emily have than Sarah?
