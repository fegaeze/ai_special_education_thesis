### Combine: The whole is unknown

1. The largest toy soldier battle on the playground involved Tim's 58 soldiers, Ramon's 64 soldiers, and Nadine's 80 soldiers. How many toy soldiers participated in that battle?
2. I bought 4 red apples and 3 green apples. How many apples did I buy?
3. A hungry hummingbird drinks nectar from 85 pink flowers and 95 blue flowers. How many flowers did the hummingbird drink nectar from?
4. Roberta has 8 Dr. Seuss books. She also has 3 Leo Lionni books. How many books does she have altogether?
5. We have 8 long-sleeve shirts and 12 short-sleeve shirts. How many shirts do we have?
6. Harry scored 34 points on his French written exam and 32 points on his oral exam. What is his total score?
7. At the talent show, 67 first-year students and 49 second-year students came to watch. How many students were at the show?
8. Alexander ran his first sprint in 18 seconds and his second sprint in 24 seconds. What was his total time?
9. Susan's grandpa was fibbing again: “your grandma and I, together, we are over 150 years old!” If her grandpa is 71, and her grandma is 68, what's the real sum of their ages?
