### Change-Join: The whole is unknown

1. There are 16 mice playing in a field. Eight more mice come and start playing, too. How many mice are playing in the field?
2. Fifty-two ladybugs are crawling around in the garden. Fifty-six more ladybugs join them. How many ladybugs are in the garden altogether?
3. Steve loses 4 teeth in first grade. In second grade, he loses 3 more teeth. How many teeth does Steve lose?
4. Dr. Bright was 21 years old when he invented a robot. It took him another 43 years to invent something else. How old was he then?
5. Ten balloons are tied to a bench. Mr. Dylan ties 8 more balloons to the bench. How many balloons are tied to the bench now?
6. A soccer team scores 3 goals in the first half of the game. In the second half, they score 5 more goals. How many goals does the soccer team score in total?
7. Eight girls are building a sandcastle. Five more girls come to help them. How many girls are building the sandcastle now?
8. Ivan has 54 chickens on the farm. His dad buys 60 more chickens and 20 geese. How many name tags will Ivan need for all the chickens?
9. A bakery sells 42 loaves of bread in the morning and 35 more in the afternoon. How many loaves of bread are sold in total?
